## A note on the data

Every number in `data/scenarios/` is **synthetic**, generated by
`analysis/generate_scenarios.py` from stated assumptions. Allele frequencies and ancestry
proportions are calibrated against published aggregate references (gnomAD, 1000 Genomes,
*All of Us*), which are cited in each bundle's `provenance` block and surfaced in the
application. Effect sizes are invented for teaching legibility and are not published
estimates. No individual-level genetic data is present anywhere in this repository, and
reusers must not present these scenarios as empirical results.
